# Fact Checking

## Triggers

Use for disputed, current, high-impact, numerical, causal, or source-sensitive claims.

## Anti-triggers

Do not use for pure creative writing, subjective preference, or a claim explicitly treated as an assumption.

## Inputs

Atomic claims, dates, jurisdictions, source requirements, and the decision affected by each claim.

## Procedure

1. Split the material into independently verifiable claims.
2. Separate primary evidence, secondary reporting, and inference.
3. Seek corroborating and contradicting evidence.
4. Record verdict, confidence, date scope, and unresolved gaps.

## Observable output

A claim table with source class, supporting evidence, contrary evidence, verdict, and confidence.

## Composition

Combine with `horizontal-vertical-analysis` or `steelman-comparison`; use no more than three reasoning skills total.

## Permissions

This skill does not itself grant browsing, database, file, or network access.

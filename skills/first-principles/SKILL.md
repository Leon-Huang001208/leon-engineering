# First Principles

## Triggers

Use for novel constraints, foundational architecture, or a stalled decision dominated by convention.

## Anti-triggers

Do not discard verified repository patterns, standards, or evidence merely to appear original.

## Inputs

Objective, hard constraints, verified facts, inherited assumptions, and measurable success conditions.

## Procedure

1. State the outcome and hard constraints.
2. Separate facts from conventions and preferences.
3. Derive the smallest necessary system from the facts.
4. Compare the derivation with existing patterns and retain justified reuse.

## Observable output

A fact/assumption split, derived requirements, and a decision showing what is reused or changed and why.

## Composition

Combine with `socratic-clarification` or `minimal-experiment`; use no more than three reasoning skills total.

## Permissions

This skill grants no data, file, network, dependency, delivery, or destructive permission.

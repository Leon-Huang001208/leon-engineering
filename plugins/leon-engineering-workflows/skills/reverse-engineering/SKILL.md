# Reverse Engineering

## Triggers

Use to explain why a system, artifact, or outcome works from observable evidence.

## Anti-triggers

Do not use for unconstrained creation, unsupported speculation, or source reconstruction without authority.

## Inputs

Observed outcome, available artifacts, comparison baseline, and the decisions the user hopes to reproduce.

## Procedure

1. Inventory observable features and evidence.
2. Infer candidate mechanisms and label confidence.
3. Identify necessary, enabling, and incidental factors.
4. Convert high-confidence mechanisms into replicable tests or design rules.

## Observable output

An evidence-to-mechanism map, confidence labels, and a list of reproducible levers.

## Composition

Combine with `fact-checking` or `minimal-experiment`; use no more than three reasoning skills total.

## Permissions

This skill grants no data, file, network, dependency, delivery, or destructive permission.

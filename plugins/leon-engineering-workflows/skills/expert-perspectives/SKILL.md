# Expert Perspectives

## Triggers

Use for cross-functional decisions involving materially different incentives, standards, or failure modes.

## Anti-triggers

Do not use personas as authority, invent expert consensus, or multiply perspectives when one fact suffices.

## Inputs

Decision, affected disciplines, shared evidence, stakeholder incentives, and required synthesis.

## Procedure

1. Select only perspectives with distinct decision relevance.
2. Apply the same evidence and decision question to each.
3. Surface assumptions, failure modes, and preferred tradeoffs per perspective.
4. Synthesize agreements, conflicts, and the owner of each unresolved decision.

## Observable output

A perspective matrix and synthesis that distinguishes evidence from professional judgment.

## Composition

Combine with `steelman-comparison` or `fact-checking`; use no more than three reasoning skills total.

## Permissions

This skill grants no professional authority or new operational permission.

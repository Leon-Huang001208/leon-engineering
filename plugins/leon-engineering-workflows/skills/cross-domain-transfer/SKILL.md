# Cross-domain Transfer

## Triggers

Use when the source and target domains share causal structure, constraints, or feedback dynamics.

## Anti-triggers

Do not transfer based on surface resemblance, prestige, or vocabulary alone.

## Inputs

Target problem, source-domain pattern, candidate shared mechanism, and domain-specific constraints.

## Procedure

1. Describe both domains without analogy language.
2. Map causal roles and constraints one by one.
3. Identify breaks, missing variables, and unsafe transfers.
4. Translate only surviving mechanisms into a testable target-domain hypothesis.

## Observable output

A structural mapping, explicit analogy breaks, and a bounded transfer hypothesis.

## Composition

Combine with `first-principles` or `minimal-experiment`; use no more than three reasoning skills total.

## Permissions

This skill grants no data, file, network, dependency, delivery, or destructive permission.
